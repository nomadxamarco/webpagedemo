ABOUT ME

I'm Nomad x Amarco, a New York-based photographer with over a decade behind the camera. For me, photography has never been just about taking a picture. It's about perspective; the way a familiar place can feel completely different when you stop long enough to really see it.

I'm drawn to people, places, movement, and the moments in between. The things that might otherwise go unnoticed. A passing expression. A certain kind of light. A street at the right time of day. A moment that exists for only a second.

That's the idea behind Allegory of the Camera. The belief that a camera can tell a story beyond what is immediately in front of the lens.

NOMAD X AMARCO is my exploration of that idea.

A collection of places, people, perspectives, and moments as I encounter them.

This is more than a portfolio.

It's how i see the world.
